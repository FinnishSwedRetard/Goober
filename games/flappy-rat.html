Flappy Rat

A tiny sewer rat with mutant wings has 100 seconds to escape the rising poop tide. The wings flap on their own, so focus on weaving around sewer pipes, slowing down under dookie-dropping pipes, and collecting charges for a powerful boost. Stay airborne, keep moving, and make it to the exit!
